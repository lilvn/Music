# Privacy Policy

**App:** 485 Music
**Last updated:** June 24, 2026

485 Music is a music player for your own self‑hosted [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org) media server. Your privacy is simple to describe because the app is built around one idea: **your data stays between your device and the server you choose.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** 485 Music does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information to the developer or to any third party. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third‑party SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

To connect to your server, the app saves the following **only on your device**:

- The server address you enter
- Your username
- An access token issued by your server after you sign in

This information is used solely to communicate with the Jellyfin server you specify. It never leaves your device except to talk to that server. Signing out removes it.

## Information sent to your server

When you sign in, your username and password are sent **directly to the Jellyfin server you entered** so it can authenticate you. After that, the app exchanges normal requests with your server to browse and play your library (album art, track lists, playback progress, favorites, etc.). The developer has no access to your server or any of this traffic.

## Third parties

485 Music does not integrate any third‑party analytics, advertising, or data‑collection services. The only network connections the app makes are to the server address you provide.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
