# 485 Music — Support

485 Music is a native iOS player for your own self‑hosted [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org) music library.

## Requirements

- An iPhone running iOS 26 or later
- Access to a Jellyfin server with music in its library

## Getting started

1. Open the app.
2. On the sign‑in screen enter your **server address** (for example `https://music.example.com` or `http://192.168.1.10:8096`), your **username**, and your **password**.
3. Tap **Sign In**. Your library appears once you're connected.

## Frequently asked questions

**It won't connect / "Couldn't reach the server."**
Check that the server address is reachable from your phone (try opening it in Safari), that you included the port if your server uses one, and that you're on a network that can reach the server. If the server uses `http` rather than `https`, enter the full `http://…` address.

**"Incorrect username or password."**
Re‑enter your Jellyfin credentials. These are the same ones you use to log in to your Jellyfin server.

**Where is my music stored?**
On your own Jellyfin server. The app only plays what your server provides — it doesn't host or upload anything.

**Does the app collect any of my data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). Your credentials stay on your device and are only used to talk to your server.

**AirPlay / Bluetooth playback.**
Playback continues when you switch to AirPlay or Bluetooth. Unplugging a wired/Bluetooth device pauses playback, matching the system Music app.
